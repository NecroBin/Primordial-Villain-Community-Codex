Quinlan's Classes

Quinlan's major classes. The Primordial Villain is his primary hub—housing Necromancy and the Bloodfather under its Harbinger of Ruin subclass. The Harbinger of Aeons is his elemental combat class. The Primordial Subjugator is his anomalous second Primordial class.

Primordial Villain

Unique Primary Class Hub
▼
Quinlan's primary class. Functions as both a massive stat multiplier and the gateway to all his dark powers. The stacked bonuses from this class are the core reason Quinlan fights on par with Level 74s despite being in the low 50s.
Passive Stat Bonuses
+25% Core Stats
Agility, Strength, Vitality, Magic
+50% Resource Pools
Health Points, Mana Points
Active Spell
[Warp Gate]
Spammable teleport that can be expanded for group transport. Only one gate can exist at a time—no portal pairs. Quinlan's primary mobility tool.
Subclass: Harbinger of Ruin
The combat subclass gating access to evil-aligned class trees. Unlike other classes where Free Skill Points unlock spells, Harbinger of Ruin requires meeting specific in-world conditions to unlock each branch.
[Soul Reaper] — Weapon
Pitch-black saber forged by Björn, the Primordial Blacksmith. Emits ghostly pale flames when soul powers activate. Channels all necromantic abilities. Quinlan's only physical weapon.
Necromancy Unlocked
[Eternal Damnation]
Harvests souls from slain enemies and stores them in the Soul Reaper.
[March of the Damned]
Mass summon up to 100 lesser soul constructs. Units inherit minor traits from the original soul but are stripped of individuality. 24-hour lifespan. Follow basic formation and combat orders. Scales with Necromancy Rank.
[Awaken]
Mass-summon Elite souls. Quinlan's Elites can be awakened simultaneously in a single cast. Each retains advanced cognitive function, their original class abilities, and operates semi-autonomously. Led by Scar, a restored assassin who serves as the soul army's general.
[Soul Fusion]
Ritualistic merge of 3+ lesser souls into an Elite soul, strengthening its stats and abilities. Consumed souls are permanently destroyed. Chance to unlock latent traits or dormant skills.
[Necromantic Codex]
Passive progress tracker bound via Soul Reaper initiation. Automatically records soul acquisitions, ritual outcomes, and summoning data.
Bloodfather Unlocked
SPOILER — First revealed Ch 1638, mechanics Ch 1663
A family-type primordial-tier class. Acquired when the Blood Mage integration failed—the Abyssal Genesis Physique rejected the mortal template and authored this class in its place. Its measure is not the strength of its holder but the strength of the family bonded to its holder. The first class in history to generate XP without a source kill.
“Bind only those you are willing to bleed for.”
Bond Pool
Generates +20% bonus XP from any bonded family member's kills—created from nothing, not redistributed. The killer keeps 100%. Distributed inverse to level, pulling the weakest members up fastest. Stacks with Blessed Seed.
Crimson Reservoir
Tactical reserve integrated into Quinlan's body (formerly the Sangomar Line flask-stone). Refills through bonded kills. Bonded can draw from the Reservoir for various benefits. Cannot heal.
Mark of the Bloodfather
Unique demonic sigil placed on each bonded, drawn in Quinlan's blood. The bonded chooses placement and can refuse. Each mark is a conduit—a physical manifestation of the bond between Bloodfather and bonded.
The Living Bond
Quinlan feels every kill and every wound across any distance. In combat, intent flows between all bonded without speech—the family operates as a single organism.
The Cost
Two bond types: Beloved (bound in body and heart) and Champions (bound in trust and war). A Beloved's death costs Quinlan permanent stat degradation and class ceiling reduction. A Champion's death causes temporary reduction in other Champions' mark gifts. Every binding costs literal blood.

The Harbinger of Aeons

Unique Mythic Hybrid — SpellBlade
▼
“You are no longer merely a mage, no longer a martial artist, no longer a survivor. You are the elemental storm that walks, the warrior-savant who forges worlds—and sunders them. Fire is your fury, Water is your will, Earth is your resolve, Wind is your freedom. You wield the elements not as tools, but as living extensions of your soul, channeled through blade, through spell, through sheer indomitable will. You are change incarnate, a living epoch. The Harbinger of Aeons is not merely a title. It is a destiny. A burden. A promise. Where you walk, the old world ends, and the new one begins. The Harbinger of Aeons is the herald of a new age.”

Evolved from Elemental Sovereign → Avatar of the Elements → Harbinger of Aeons. Quinlan wields seven elements—Earth, Wind, Fire, Water, Ice, Lightning, and Magma—and switches between them instantly with zero cooldown or transition time. Most mages specialize in one or two. Morgana, a Level 74 who spent centuries mastering elements, was stunned: “All seven… It took me hundreds of years to get to where you are. And you switch between them instantly! No cooldown, no transition, seven elements rotating on demand.” He learned martial arts in the world of Zhenwu, transforming from a strict mage into a true SpellBlade.

Class Traits
Aeon's Path
Class evolves with every divine concept nurtured—Love, Wrath, Sacrifice, Freedom, or others yet unnamed. Final form determined by soul and choices.
Aether-Threaded Body Silent Casting
Quinlan's body is a living conduit of elemental will. He does not chant, gesture, or incant—he expresses power through pure will and motion, bypassing traditional casting entirely. Elements respond to his desire alone: fire dances across his knuckles without being summoned, earth rises at a flick of his wrist. Silent casting is extraordinarily rare in this world—first revealed in Ch 162 when he levitated using [Airwalk] while a silence spell suppressed all sound, leaving onlookers gawking in disbelief. He can also layer multiple elements simultaneously, combining fire behind wind for accelerated lances, magma with water for hardened barriers, or threading lightning through gaps in earth constructs.
Elemental Stance
Switch between elemental forms mid-combat. Each grants unique martial and magical buffs.

Primordial Subjugator

Unique 2nd Primordial Class Anomaly
▼

System Anomaly

Quinlan is the only Primordial in history to possess two Primordial classes. The system itself flagged this as unprecedented.

Evolved from the Slave Master class. Enables the subjugation and empowerment of others through bonds written into the soul. Everyone in Quinlan's inner circle is technically his slave, but the bond carries real benefits and is not treated as degrading among his lovers. Each girl has a personalized slave collar styled to her personality.
Abilities
[Subjugate]
Force a contract upon those beaten by Quinlan. No collar medium required, unlike the Slave Contract spell. Willing participants can also be enslaved voluntarily. Limited subjugations per time frame.
[Master's Link]
Telepathic communication with all slaves. Denoted by <angle brackets> in dialogue. Works across any distance.
[Overlord's Eyes]
See through any subjugated underling's eyes from any distance.
[Overlord's Sacrifice]
When the Subjugator receives a fatal wound, all damage transfers to a designated subjugated target—only if the target can sustain the wounds. Quinlan is fully healed. 7-day cooldown.
[Overlord's Magnanimity]
Transfer up to 90% of stats to designated Subjugated targets. When split across multiple targets, effectiveness is reduced. Non-combat buff.
[Slave Monitor]
View and interact with slaves' status screens remotely.
