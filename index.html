About the Author

I’m from the small country of Hungary (no, I’m not hungry) and I suppose I sport an overly vivid imagination.

While reading other stories, I kept getting more and more irritated, imagining how I’d have done things differently. This, combined with my general boredom about the mundaneness of life combined into the decision of taking matters into my own hands, and the rest is history.

Now, I’m fortunate enough to call myself a full-time author whose only job is to publish his thoughts onto the world wide web.
