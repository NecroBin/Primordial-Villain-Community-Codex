About the Author

NecroBin — writer of the Necroverse

I’m from the small country of Hungary (no, I’m not hungry) and I suppose I sport an overly vivid imagination.

While reading other stories, I kept getting more and more irritated, imagining how I’d have done things differently. This, paired with my general boredom, led to the decision of taking matters into my own hands.

And, well… here we are: two ongoing novels, thousands of chapters, and a community that keeps surprising me. The Necroverse is what I call the growing collection — Primordial Villain with a Slave Harem was its first entry, Demonic Pornstar System the second, and it won’t stop there.
